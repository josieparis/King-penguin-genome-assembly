# 10 — Non-coding RNA annotation

| Script | Tag | What |
|---|---|---|
| `01_infernal_cmscan_rfam.sh` | original | Infernal 1.1.2 `cmscan --cut_ga --rfam --nohmmonly --clanin` against **Rfam 15** on the curated hap1; `-Z` = 2 × genome length / 10⁶ (29054.15 from `esl-seqstat`). Low-scoring overlapping hits removed (`KP-genome-rfam.deoverlapped.tblout`) and E-value > 1e-5 discarded, then grouped: 281 miRNA, 253 snoRNA, 60 snRNA, 341 rRNA, 16 known lncRNA, 81 cis-regulatory elements, 6 ribozymes (Supplementary Tables 8–9) |
| `02_rnammer.sh` | original | RNAmmer 1.2 `-S euk -m lsu,ssu,tsu` to confirm the rRNA subunits |
| `03_trnascan_se.sh` | original | tRNAscan-SE 2.0.9 on the soft-masked genome → 358 tRNAs after the Cove score > 50 filter of Ottenburghs et al. 2021 (`results/trnascan_KP_stats.txt`); the same run on *S. humboldti* GCA_027474245.1 gives 389 (`results/trnascan_S_humboldti_stats.txt`, Supplementary Table 10) |
| `lncRNA/04_lncrna_pipeline.sh` | RECONSTRUCTED (minimap2 line from log) | novel lncRNAs: king penguin de novo transcriptome (Paris et al. 2025) aligned with `minimap2 -ax splice`; StringTie 2.2.1 guided by the annotation (`-G better_UTR.gtf -A -C`; command recorded in the GTF header); unannotated transcripts kept (`extract_novel.py`, `clean_headers.py`); DIAMOND blastx vs the annotated proteins and UniProtKB 2025_01 (Swiss-Prot + TrEMBL) removes coding transcripts; CPC2, CPAT 3.0.5 (species-specific hexamer table and logit model — `CPAT_make_logitModel.R`, `CPAT_output.R`, `results/CPAT_run_info.log`) and PLEK; transcripts > 200 nt called non-coding by all three → **164 lncRNAs**, mean 673 bp (`results/final_lncRNA_gene_list.tsv`); `rename_lncRNA_headers.py` names the released FASTA (`bAptPat1-lncRNA-annotation.*`) |
